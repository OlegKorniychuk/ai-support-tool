title: How readings are personalized
tags: readings, horoscope, birth chart, disclaimer
---
Every personalized reading in the app is built from your birth date, time and place, combined with current astrological positions.

Your birth details form your birth chart, a map of where the sun, moon and planets were at the moment you were born. The app calculates your birth chart from what you enter in Profile → Birth details; the more precise your birth time, the more detailed the chart, which is why the app asks for time down to the minute where you know it.

Daily horoscopes and other readings on the Readings tab combine your birth chart with the current positions of the sun, moon and planets to generate guidance relevant to that day or period. If you don't know your exact birth time, readings use a simplified chart based on date and place alone; see "Edit birth date/time/place" for how that setting works.

Entertainment and guidance disclaimer: readings are intended for entertainment and general guidance only. They are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or mental health advice, and no reading should be used as the sole basis for a major life decision.

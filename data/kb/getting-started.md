The app combines daily horoscopes, personalized astrology readings, and paid one-on-one sessions with astrology experts, all built around your own birth details.

The app has four main tabs:
- Home: your daily horoscope feed and quick links to today's guidance.
- Readings: personalized reports built from your birth chart, including compatibility and life-area breakdowns.
- Experts: browse astrology experts, check their profiles and ratings, and book a session.
- Profile: your account, birth details, subscription, payment methods, notifications and support.

To get started:
1. Create an account with an email address or a supported social sign-in.
2. Enter your birth date, time and place in Profile → Birth details. An accurate birth time gives the most precise readings.
3. Open the Home tab to see your first daily horoscope.
4. Explore the Readings tab for a deeper look at your birth chart.
5. When you're ready for personal guidance, open the Experts tab to browse and book a session with an astrology expert.

A Premium subscription is optional and unlocks unlimited personalized readings; expert sessions are booked and paid for separately from any subscription. See "Subscription plans" and "Booking an expert session" for details.

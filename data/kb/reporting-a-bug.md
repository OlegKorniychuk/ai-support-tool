Clear bug reports get fixed faster, so include as much detail as you can.

What to include:
- Device model and operating system version.
- The app's version number, found in Profile → Help → About.
- Exact steps to reproduce the problem, in order.
- What you expected to happen versus what actually happened.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Where to send it: open Profile → Help → Report a problem, or Profile → Help → Contact support and choose the "Bug report" category. Describe the issue and attach any screenshots.

What happens next: your report is logged in our internal tracking system and reviewed by the product team. Common or high-impact bugs are prioritized for upcoming app updates. We don't promise a fix timeline or individual follow-up for every report, but we may reach out through Profile → Help → Contact support if we need more information to reproduce the issue.

If the app crashes before you can even open it, see "App won't open / crashes" for recovery steps first, then report the bug once the app is usable again.

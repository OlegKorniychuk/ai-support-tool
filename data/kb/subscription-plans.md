A Premium subscription unlocks the app's full personalized content on top of the free daily horoscope.

Plans: Premium is available billed monthly or billed annually. Both plans include the same features; the annual plan has a lower effective monthly cost than paying month to month.

What's included with Premium:
- Unlimited personalized readings on the Readings tab, including full birth chart and compatibility reports (the free tier includes one daily horoscope only).
- An ad-free experience throughout the app.
- Priority display of new report types on the Readings tab as they're added.

Expert sessions are booked and paid for separately from a Premium subscription; see "Booking an expert session".

Renewal: Premium renews automatically at the end of each billing period, monthly or annually depending on your plan, at the then-current price, until you cancel. To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

You can view your current plan, renewal date and price in Profile → Subscription. To change plans or cancel, see "Cancel a subscription".

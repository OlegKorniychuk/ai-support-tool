id: delete-account
title: Delete account and data
tags: delete account, privacy, data deletion
---
Deleting your account permanently removes your data from the app; this cannot be undone, so review what's affected first.

What's lost: your birth details, reading history, expert-session history, and ratings and reviews you've left, along with your login credentials. None of this can be recovered once deletion completes.

Before you delete: cancel any active Premium subscription first, separately from deleting your account. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed through the App Store, Google Play, or the web; if you skip this step, billing can continue even after your account is gone. See "Cancel a subscription" for how to cancel on each platform.

How to delete your account:
1. Open Profile → Privacy → Delete account.
2. Confirm you understand the deletion is permanent.
3. Confirm again to submit the request.

Timing: your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days of your request. Some records, such as transaction records needed for accounting or legal purposes, may be retained for longer as required by law, separately from your personal profile and reading history.

If you only want to stop notifications or step back temporarily, consider "Cancel a subscription" or "Notifications" instead of deleting your account.

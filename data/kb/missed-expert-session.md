title: Missed or cancelled expert session
tags: expert no-show, cancellation, session credit
---
Sometimes a booked session doesn't happen as planned, either because the expert doesn't join or because the customer does not.

If the expert misses or cancels: your session credit is returned automatically within 24 hours, with no action needed from you. You can use it to book a new session with the same or a different expert.

If you, the customer, miss the session:
- If you cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before the session's start time, no credit is lost; see "Booking an expert session".
- If you don't join the session within the first 10 minutes after its start time, it counts as a customer no-show. In this case, the session credit is not returned, since the expert made the time available and was present.

If you believe an expert ended a session early or didn't show up despite our records indicating otherwise, contact support from Profile → Help → Contact support with the date and time of the session so we can check the session log before deciding.

For concerns about an expert's conduct during a session, rather than timing, see "Expert code of conduct".

Experts on the app agree to a code of conduct designed to keep sessions professional and safe.

Experts may not:
- Ask you to pay them outside the app, through any personal or third-party payment method.
- Ask for or exchange personal contact details in order to continue the relationship outside the app.
- Guarantee specific outcomes, such as promising a certain event will or won't happen.
- Use offensive, discriminatory or harassing language, including comments about your appearance unrelated to the reading.
- Pressure you to book additional sessions or spend more than you're comfortable with.

If any of this happens during a session, you can end the session immediately; you are not required to stay on the call.

How to report it:
1. Open Profile → Help → Contact support.
2. Choose the "Expert conduct" category.
3. Include the expert's name, the date and time of the session, and what happened.

Reports are reviewed by our trust and safety team. Depending on the outcome, an expert may be warned, suspended or permanently removed from the app. We take these reports seriously even when only one customer has reported a given expert.

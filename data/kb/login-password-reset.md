title: Login and password reset
tags: login, password, sign in, account access
---
Trouble signing in usually comes down to your password or sign-in method, and most cases are fixed from the login screen itself.

To log in, use the email and password you registered with, or a supported social sign-in (Apple or Google) — whichever you used when creating your account.

To reset a forgotten password:
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes; request a new one if it expires.
4. Choose a new password of at least 8 characters and confirm it.
5. Return to the app and log in with the new password.

If you signed up with a social sign-in and don't remember setting a password, use "Forgot password" with the same email; the app will let you set a password for direct email login as well.

If you don't receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then contact support from Profile → Help → Contact support. For app crashes or the app failing to open at all, see "App won't open / crashes" instead — that is a separate issue from login errors.

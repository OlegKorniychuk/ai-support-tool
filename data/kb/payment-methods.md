The app accepts several ways to pay for a Premium subscription or an expert session.

Supported methods:
- Credit or debit card, entered directly in the app.
- App Store billing, for purchases made on iOS.
- Google Play billing, for purchases made on Android.
- PayPal, available when paying through the app's website.

Updating a card:
1. Open Profile → Payment methods.
2. Tap the card on file, then Edit, or tap Add payment method to add a new one.
3. Enter the new card details and Save.

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are managed in your device's store account settings instead, not in the app.

Failed payments: if a renewal or session payment fails, for example because of an expired card, we retry the charge automatically for up to 3 days. During this time your Premium access continues. If all retries fail, Premium access ends and the subscription is marked as unpaid; update your payment method and resubscribe from Profile → Subscription to restore access.

For a charge that looks wrong rather than failed, see "Duplicate or unauthorized charge". For cancelling instead of updating payment details, see "Cancel a subscription".

title: Duplicate or unauthorized charge
tags: duplicate charge, unauthorized charge, billing, refund
---
If you see two charges for what should be one payment, or a charge you don't recognize, here's how to sort it out.

First, check the charge:
1. Open your bank or card statement, or on iOS/Android check your App Store or Google Play purchase history.
2. Note the exact date, amount and description of each charge.
3. Charges can take up to 48 hours to fully appear or disappear on a statement, so a charge that looks duplicated might resolve on its own within that window.

If a genuine duplicate or unauthorized charge remains after 48 hours:
1. Open Profile → Help → Contact support and choose the "Billing" category.
2. Include the dates and amounts of both charges (or the unrecognized charge) and your account email.
3. Support will check our billing records against what you describe.

Once a duplicate or unauthorized charge is confirmed, it is refunded in full to your original payment method, processed within 5–7 business days, the same timing as other billing refunds.

If the charge is for a subscription you meant to cancel but didn't in time, see "Cancel a subscription" and "Subscription plans" for how renewal timing works.

tags: privacy, data, account, security

The app stores certain personal data in order to provide personalized readings and support, and handles it carefully.

What's stored:
- Account details: email address and login information.
- Birth data: the date, time and place you enter in Profile → Birth details.
- Activity: reading history, booked expert sessions and ratings you've left.
- Payment metadata, such as the last four digits of a card and transaction records; full card numbers are handled by our payment processors, not stored on our servers.

Why it's stored: birth data powers your personalized readings; account and activity data lets you pick up where you left off across devices and lets support help you when you reach out; payment metadata is needed to process charges and refunds.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Where to review your privacy settings: open Profile → Privacy to see what's stored about you and to manage the options available there, including requesting a copy of your data.

To remove your data entirely rather than just review it, see "Delete account and data", which explains what deleting your account does and doesn't remove.

title: Feature requests and feedback
tags: feedback, feature request, roadmap
---
Ideas for new features or changes are welcome and help shape future updates, even though we can't act on every request.

How to send feedback:
1. Open Profile → Help → Send feedback.
2. Describe the feature or change you'd like to see and why it would help you.
3. Submit the form.

What happens to it: feedback is collected and reviewed by the product team alongside requests from other users. Requests that come up often are weighed more heavily when planning future work.

No promises on timelines: we don't confirm whether or when a specific request will be built, and we don't send individual updates on the status of a submitted idea. Feature requests are different from bug reports: if something in the app is broken or not working as designed, use "Reporting a bug" instead so it reaches the right team.

You can submit as many feature requests as you like. There's no need to submit the same idea more than once; similar requests are grouped together automatically when reviewed, so one clear submission is enough.

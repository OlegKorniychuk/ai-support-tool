title: Cancel a subscription
tags: cancel, subscription, premium, billing
---
You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time; how you do it depends on where you subscribed.

On iOS: open the Settings app on your device → tap your name → Subscriptions → the app → Cancel Subscription.

On Android: open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → the app → Cancel.

On the web: log in at the app's website, go to Profile → Subscription, and tap Cancel Subscription.

Timing: to avoid being charged for the next cycle, cancel at least 48 hours before your renewal date. Cancelling closer to the renewal date may not stop that charge in time.

What happens after you cancel: you keep Premium access until the end of your current paid billing period; it does not end immediately. After that date, your account reverts to the free tier, and personalized readings beyond the daily horoscope are no longer available. Your birth details, past readings and session history are kept.

Cancelling does not automatically trigger a refund for the current period; see "Refund policy" if you believe you're eligible for one. Cancelling your subscription does not cancel or affect any booked expert sessions.

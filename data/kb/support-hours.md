tags: support, contact, hours, response time

Support is available to help with anything the Help Center articles don't cover.

Hours: our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 UTC. Requests submitted outside these hours are queued and handled the next business day.

Typical response times: expect a first response within 1 business day for most requests submitted during support hours. Billing and account-safety issues, such as a duplicate charge or an expert conduct report, are generally reviewed first. Response times may be longer around weekends and holidays.

Channels: the only support channel is in-app, through Profile → Help → Contact support. From there you can choose a category, such as Bug report, Billing, Expert conduct, or General question, and describe your issue. If the app won't open at all, you can reach the same contact form from a browser at the app's website; see "App won't open / crashes".

There is no phone line or live chat; all requests go through the Contact support form so they're tracked and routed to the right team. For the fastest response, include your account email and as much detail as described in "Reporting a bug" if your issue is a bug.

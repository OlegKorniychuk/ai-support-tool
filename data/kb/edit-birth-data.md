title: Edit birth date, time and place
tags: birth data, profile, readings, birth chart
---
Your birth date, time and place are the foundation of every personalized reading in the app, so keeping them accurate matters.

To edit your birth details:
1. Open Profile → Birth details.
2. Tap the field you want to change: date, time or place.
3. For place, search by city and select the matching result so the app can use the correct time zone.
4. If you don't know your exact birth time, turn on "I don't know my birth time" instead of guessing; readings will use a simplified, less precise chart.
5. Tap Save.

Effect on readings: after you save, the Home and Readings tabs refresh with updated content. This usually happens within a few minutes, the next time you open those tabs. Your birth chart, and all future daily horoscopes, compatibility reports and expert-session context, use the new details.

Readings you already saved or received before the change keep their original text and are not rewritten retroactively; only new readings use the updated birth data.

Birth details can be edited as often as needed. If you accidentally enter the wrong place and can't find it in the search results, contact support from Profile → Help → Contact support for assistance.

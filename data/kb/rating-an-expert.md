title: Rating an expert
tags: rating, review, experts, feedback
---
After each expert session, you're asked to leave a rating so other customers can make informed choices.

To rate a session:
1. Open Experts → My sessions and select the completed session, or use the prompt shown right after the session ends.
2. Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars.
3. Optionally add a written review describing your experience.
4. Submit.

Your rating and review appear on the expert's public profile alongside their average rating. Reviews are shown with your first name only; your full name and account details are never shown to other customers.

Editing a rating: open Experts → My sessions → the session → Edit rating. You can change your star rating or review within 7 days of submitting it. After 7 days, the rating is final and can no longer be edited from the app; contact support from Profile → Help → Contact support if you believe a rating needs to be removed, for example if it was left by mistake.

Ratings you leave are separate from reporting an expert for misconduct; for that, see "Expert code of conduct".

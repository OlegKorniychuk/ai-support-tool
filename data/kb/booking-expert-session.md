Booking time with an astrology expert lets you get guidance tailored to a specific question, beyond your automated readings.

To book a session:
1. Open the Experts tab and browse expert profiles, filtering by specialty, rating or price.
2. Open an expert's profile to see their available time slots.
3. Select a slot and pay for the session. Payment issues you one session credit, which is applied automatically to that booking.
4. You'll receive a confirmation and, if enabled, a reminder before the session — see "Notifications".

Rescheduling or cancelling: open Experts → My sessions → select the session → Reschedule or Cancel. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before the session's start time. Changes made after that point are treated as a customer cancellation; see "Missed or cancelled expert session" for what happens then.

If the expert misses or cancels the session, your session credit is returned within 3 business days so you can book another expert.

Session credits are used only for booking expert sessions and are separate from a Premium subscription; see "Subscription plans" for what a subscription includes.

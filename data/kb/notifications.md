The app can send you push notifications for your daily horoscope and for expert-session reminders; you control both from within the app and from your device.

To manage notifications:
1. Open Profile → Notifications.
2. Toggle "Daily horoscope" on or off. When on, you'll get a push notification once a day with a link to that day's horoscope.
3. Tap "Daily horoscope time" to change when it arrives. The default is 8:00 AM in your device's local time; you can set it to any time you prefer.
4. Toggle "Session reminders" on or off to control reminders before an upcoming booked expert session.

If you don't receive notifications even with these turned on, check that notifications for the app are allowed in your device's system settings (Settings → Notifications on iOS, or App settings → Notifications on Android); the in-app toggles only work if the device also allows them.

Turning off "Daily horoscope" does not remove the horoscope itself from the Home tab; it only stops the push notification. You can turn notifications back on at any time from the same screen.

Refunds for the Premium subscription are available within a limited window after purchase.

Eligibility: you can request a refund within 14 days of the original purchase or renewal charge. Requests made after 14 days are not eligible under this policy, though see "Duplicate or unauthorized charge" if the charge itself looks wrong rather than a change of mind.

How to request a refund:
1. Open Profile → Help → Contact support.
2. Choose the "Billing" category.
3. Include the approximate date of the charge and the reason for your request.

Processing time: once approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. The money is returned to your original payment method; we cannot send a refund to a different card, account or method.

Refunds are issued for the current billing period's charge only; they don't cover earlier renewals. Requesting a refund does not automatically cancel your subscription — cancel separately from Profile → Subscription (see "Cancel a subscription") if you don't want it to renew again.

Expert session payments follow a different process; see "Missed or cancelled expert session" and "Booking an expert session" for how those are handled.

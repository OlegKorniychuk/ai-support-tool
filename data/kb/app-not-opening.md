title: App won't open or keeps crashing
tags: crash, won't open, update, reinstall, troubleshooting
---
When the app crashes on launch or won't open at all, a few basic steps resolve most cases.

1. Update the app: open the App Store or Google Play, check for a pending update for the app, and install it.
2. Restart your device: a full restart clears temporary issues that simply closing the app doesn't.
3. Reopen the app and confirm whether the crash still happens.
4. Reinstall the app: uninstall it, then reinstall from the App Store or Google Play.

Your data is kept: your account, birth details, readings, session history and subscription are stored on our servers, not only on your device, so reinstalling and logging back in restores everything. Local-only data, such as an in-progress unsent note, is not kept.

If the app still won't open after updating, restarting and reinstalling, contact support from a browser or another device (since the app itself won't open) via Profile → Help → Contact support on the web, and include your device model, operating system version, and when the crashes started. See "Reporting a bug" for what other details help us fix it faster.
